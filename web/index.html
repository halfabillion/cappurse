CapPurse

Give anyone a spending cap they cannot exceed. Funds go from your vault straight to the merchant, or the payment fails and nothing moves.

Alice, Bob and a cafe

One click runs the story, with a plain-language pass or fail after each step. Or walk it yourself with the forms.

  1. 1 Alice opens a purse and deposits 1,000 TARI
  2. 2 She grants Bob a 100 TARI cap, cafe and vendor only
  3. 3 Bob pays the cafe 40 — it clears, 60 remain
  4. 4 Bob tries 70 — rejected, balances unchanged
  5. 5 An unlisted shop is rejected
  6. 6 After 50 epochs the cap refills; Bob pays the vendor 100
  7. 7 Alice revokes Bob, then withdraws what is left

Calling it from a transaction

These are the calls the engine tests in template/tests/engine.rs make. Owner opens and funds a purse in one transaction:

test.transaction()
    .allocate_component_address("alloc")
    .call_function(template, "new", args![Workspace("alloc")])
    .put_last_instruction_output_on_workspace("purse")
    .call_method(owner.account, "withdraw", args![TARI_TOKEN, DEPOSIT])
    .put_last_instruction_output_on_workspace("funds")
    .call_method("purse", "deposit", args![Workspace("funds")])
    .build_and_seal(&owner.secret)

The helper spends and keeps the receipt NFT in their own account:

test.transaction()
    .call_method(purse, "spend", args![FORTY, cafe, "lunch".to_string()])
    .put_last_instruction_output_on_workspace("receipt")
    .call_method(bob.account, "deposit", args![Workspace("receipt")])
    .build_and_seal(&bob.secret)

Honest limits

  • This published page is a browser simulation. The template has not been published to Esmeralda or mainnet unless the README says so and links an explorer.
  • The template is not audited.
  • Spend amounts and memos are public. Private delegated spend is out of scope.
  • The cap is a ceiling, not a reserved lock. If the owner withdraws, spendable is min(remaining, vault).
  • Receipt NFTs prove a successful spend. They are not a refund.
  • No partial payments, no multi-owner boards, no fiat on-ramp.
