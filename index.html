CapPurse

A spend-capped allowance vault on Tari Ootle. Deposit TARI, give a helper a hard cap, and revoke it at once. The helper never holds the funds.

An owner grants a person or an agent a ceiling (with an optional period, expiry, and merchant allowlist). A payment goes from the purse vault straight to the merchant in the same transaction, or the whole thing fails and nothing moves.

This is payer-side control, not a pay-by-link invoice, a tip jar, or a membership pass.
